14-Day Refund Request Window

You may request a refund review for an initial subscription payment within 14 days of the purchase date. Submitting a request within 14 days does not automatically guarantee a refund. Because AI Resume Studio provides immediately accessible digital services and AI-generated content, refund eligibility may be limited or denied once paid features have been substantially used or consumed.

Examples of substantial use may include generating or receiving a full resume rewrite, tailored cover letter, other premium AI-generated output, or otherwise making significant use of paid subscription features.

Renewal Charges and Cancellation

Canceling a subscription stops future renewals according to the cancellation terms shown at checkout or in the billing portal. Unless otherwise stated, paid access remains available through the end of the current paid billing period.

Cancellation does not automatically create a refund for a charge that has already been processed. Subscription renewal charges are generally non-refundable once the new billing period has begun and the paid service has been available for use, except where required by law or otherwise approved under this policy.

Refund requests involving duplicate charges, billing errors, unauthorized charges, or a material technical failure that prevented access to a paid feature may be reviewed separately from the standard eligibility rules. We may request reasonable information needed to identify the transaction and verify the issue.

How to Request a Refund

Use the support/contact method provided through AI Resume Studio and include enough information to identify the transaction without sending full payment-card details. We may request additional information reasonably needed to locate the charge, confirm service usage, and review the request.

Payment Processing

Subscription payments are processed securely through Stripe. AI Resume Studio does not directly receive or store full payment-card numbers. Approved refunds are generally returned through Stripe to the original payment method.

Consumer Rights

Nothing in this Refund Policy limits or excludes any refund, cancellation, or consumer rights that cannot legally be waived under the laws that apply to you.

Approved refunds are submitted through the applicable payment processor. The time for funds to appear can vary depending on the payment method, financial institution, and payment processor.
